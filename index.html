What is a Stroke?

A stroke occurs when the blood supply to part of your brain is interrupted or reduced, depriving brain tissue of oxygen and nutrients. Within minutes, brain cells begin to die. This can be caused by a clot (ischemic stroke) or a burst blood vessel (hemo-rragic stroke). Strokes are the leading cause of long-term disability and a major cause of death worldwide.

The effects can be devastating, impacting motor skills, speech, and cognitive function. However, with timely prevention and immediate care, the outcomes can be significantly improved. Our platform provides the insights necessary to understand and mitigate this risk.

A Multi-Modal Approach

Clinical & Lifestyle (EHR)

Analyzes key metrics like age, blood pressure, BMI, and lifestyle factors to establish a baseline long-term risk.

Genetic Predisposition

Scans your genetic report for thousands of known markers (SNPs) associated with stroke, revealing your inherited risk profile.

Acute MRI Findings

Uses a deep learning model (SegResNet) to analyze your DWI and ADC scans for immediate, "acute" signs of stroke-related lesions.

Know the Signs: B.E. F.A.S.T.

This tool is for risk *assessment*, not diagnosis. If you or someone you know shows any signs of a stroke, call 911 immediately.

B - Balance

Sudden loss of balance or coordination?

E - Eyes

Sudden blurred, double, or lost vision?

F - Face

Does one side of the face droop?

A - Arms

Does one arm drift downward when raised?

S - Speech

Is speech slurred or strange?

T - Time

Time is critical. Call for help immediately.
